01 — The Problem

Thermals are a gaming phone’s make-or-break

A gaming phone faces a brutal contradiction: players want sustained, high-frame-rate performance, yet under continuous heavy load the phone inevitably heats up and throttles. Get cooling wrong, and the entire flagship performance promise collapses.

Yet most cooling solutions on the market are hidden engineering compromises — clip-on fans, concealed vents. They work, but they’re neither beautiful nor emotionally meaningful. For a faith-driven gaming brand like ROG, that’s a wasted opportunity: cooling shouldn’t just be an engineering problem — it should be an experience that reinforces brand identity.

02 — The Insight

Don’t hide it — show it off

My angle was direct: a player’s loyalty to ROG comes from the ritual of "this was built for someone like me." So the cooling mechanism shouldn’t be hidden — it should be amplified into a dynamic moment worth showing off, like a supercar’s active deployable rear wing, where function and emotion land at the same time.

This direction took shape from the earliest hand sketches. As an independent proposal, I drove it from concept ideation all the way to 3D modeling, defining a "deployable, moving, visible" cooling ritual as the design’s core thesis.

03 — The Solution

Turning the cooling vent into a mechanical ritual

The final design turns the high-load cooling vent into a deployable mechanism reminiscent of a supercar’s active rear wing — when the player enters intense gameplay and attaches the cooler accessory, the rear portal physically opens, channeling cool air straight to the processor. The moment of peak performance becomes a visible, show-off-worthy piece of premium mechanics.

  • Ultimate — dynamic cooling mechanism: A deployable physical portal that turns cooling from a "hidden compromise" into an "expressed ritual."
  • Standard — lighting design: A rear film-stack lighting treatment that carries forward the previous generation's dot-matrix language, with a subtle sense of a moving logo for supercar speed aesthetics.
04 — The Impact

From a sketch to the flagship’s signature feature

Starting from my concept sketch, the direction was refined through the final rounds of proposals and — through the team’s collective effort — adopted into ASUS’s annual product roadmap, ultimately shipping as the signature cooling mechanism (AeroActive Portal) on the flagship ROG Phone 6 Ultimate: from a single sketch to the key differentiating feature defining the flagship.

  • Cooling performance: With the portal open, roughly 1 liter of cool air per second is channeled straight to the processor, dissipating heat about 9× faster than without it.
  • Measured results: In default Fan mode it holds around a 10°C drop — a result other ROG phones need Frozen Mode to reach.
This project best captures my design thesis: good design doesn’t just solve the engineering problem (cooling) — it turns that solution into an emotional experience that makes users want to be seen and show it off.
